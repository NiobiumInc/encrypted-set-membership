# "Jaymz Smyth"≈J525; "Notpresent Zzzzz"/"Robbrt Smyth" absent.

exact   | James Smith         | exact match (line 1)        | 0.95     | 1.05
exact   | Mildred Schneider   | exact match (last line)     | 0.95     | 1.05

exact   | Robbrt Smyth        | no match (typo of in-set)   | -0.01    | 0.01
